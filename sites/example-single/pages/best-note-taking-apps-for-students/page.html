Verdict

Pick Contoso Pad if you want a free app that works anywhere. Pick Northwind Notes for group study. Pick Fabrikam Journal if you write by hand.

FAQ

Do I need a paid note app as a student?

No. Free apps cover class notes for most students. Pay only for a feature you use every week, such as handwriting search.

Which note format is safest long term?

Markdown and PDF. Most apps can open them, so you can move your notes later.

Can I use one app on my phone and laptop?

Yes, if the app syncs. Check that it also works offline before an exam week.
