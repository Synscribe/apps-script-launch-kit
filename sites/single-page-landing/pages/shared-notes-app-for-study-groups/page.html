What it does

A notes app built for groups, not only for one person

Northwind Notes keeps one set of notes for the whole group. You keep control of who can edit.

Live shared notebooks

Everyone in the group edits the same notebook at the same time. Each change shows up for the others as it happens.

One notebook per course

Create a notebook for each course and split it by week. Your group always knows where the notes for a topic are.

Export to PDF and Markdown

Export any notebook when the course ends. Your notes are not locked in.

You choose who edits

Invite classmates as editors or readers. Remove access when the group changes.

Read offline

Notebooks open without internet, so you can revise on the bus. Editing needs a connection.

Free to start

Free for one user. When your group joins, plans cost US$4 per user per month.

How it works

From empty notebook to shared notes in three steps

Set it up alone, then bring in your group.

  1. Create a notebook

    Make one notebook for your course. It is free for one user, so you can set it up before anyone else joins.

  2. Invite your group

    Send a link to your classmates. Choose who can edit and who can only read.

  3. Write together

    Everyone takes notes in the same place. Before the exam, export the notebook to PDF and revise from one file.

Questions

Is Northwind Notes free?

It is free for one user. Groups pay US$4 per user per month (checked 2026-10-07).

Can I use it without internet?

You can open and read notebooks offline. Editing needs a connection.

What happens to our notes when the course ends?

Export the notebook to PDF or Markdown. Most apps can open those files later.
