How the calculator works

  1. Pick the number of people. One person uses the single-user price. Two or more use the group price per person.
  2. Pick the number of years. The calculator multiplies the yearly price by the years. It does not apply discounts, because none are published.
  3. Read the total. The total is people × price per person × years. The note under it shows the sum.
LimitsThe calculator does not include taxes, education discounts or price changes after 2026-10-07. Check the official pricing page before you pay.

FAQ

Why does Contoso Pad show US$0 for a group?

Contoso Pad has no group plan. Each person uses the free single-user plan, so the total stays at zero.

Where do the prices come from?

From the fictional pricing pages linked on the cost per year page. On a real site, link the official pricing page for every product.

Can I copy this calculator for my own site?

Yes. The form uses the .calc classes from the theme. Change the price table in the script and in the HTML table so both stay the same.
